Our Story

Niyantri Cafe began as a small neighborhood coffee nook with a big dream: to create a space where people could slow down, savor great coffee, and connect with others. Our name, Niyantri, evokes a mindful, attentive approach to every cup we craft. We source beans from small, sustainable farms and roast them to highlight distinct flavors. Every pastry is baked fresh daily, and our space is designed to feel like a welcoming, cozy living room away from home.

Our Mission

To brew thoughtfully, serve warmly, and build community one cup at a time.

Sourcing & Sustainability

We partner with farms that practice sustainable growing, fair labor, and transparent supply chains.
